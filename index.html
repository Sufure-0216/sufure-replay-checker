📁 Where to find your Fortnite replays

Fortnite saves replay files to the following default location on Windows:

C:\Users\USERNAME\AppData\Local\FortniteGame\Saved\Demos

Replace USERNAME with your Windows username.
If you don't see AppData, enable "Show hidden files".
